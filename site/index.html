Simple by design

You remain in control from scan to Trash.

Inbox Cleaner uses Gmail’s official API and deterministic rules that run on your device.

01

Connect Gmail

Sign in through Google OAuth. Your Gmail password is never shared with the extension.

02

Review suggestions

Messages are grouped by sender and labeled KEEP, REVIEW, or CLEAN using explainable local signals.

03

Confirm cleanup

Select individual senders or messages. Nothing moves to Trash until you explicitly confirm it.

Privacy first

Your inbox stays between Google and your browser.

There is no developer-operated server receiving your email data.

Processed locallyMessage metadata and snippets are classified inside the extension.
Stored briefly on your deviceThe latest scan and message cache are retained locally for up to 24 hours to reduce Gmail API usage.
Never sold or used for adsNo email data is sold, shared for advertising, or used to build advertising profiles.
Explicit actions onlyMoving messages to Trash requires a visible selection and confirmation from you.
